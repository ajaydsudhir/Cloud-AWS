## Solution
1. Log in to the AWS Management Console using the provided credentials.
2. Navigate to the EC2 service by searching for "EC2" in the search bar and selecting it from the results.
3. In the EC2 dashboard, locate the "Elastic Block Store" section in the left-hand menu and click on "Volumes."
4. Click the "Create Volume" button to initiate the volume creation process.
5. In the "Create Volume" form, fill in the following details:
   - **Volume Type:** Select `gp3` from the dropdown menu.
   - **Size (GiB):** Enter `2` to specify the size of the volume.
6. To name the volume, click on the "Tags" tab and add a tag with the following details:
   - **Key:** `Name`
   - **Value:** `datacenter-volume` 
7. Click the "Create Volume" button to finalize the creation of the volume.

## Notes
- `Elastic Block Store (EBS)` volumes are used to provide persistent block storage, additional storage capacity, and enhanced performance for EC2 instances.
- The `gp3` volume type is a general-purpose SSD that offers a balance of price and performance, making it suitable for a wide range of workloads.
