An IAM user named `iamuser_javed` and a policy named `iampolicy_javed` already exist. Attach the IAM policy `iampolicy_javed` to the IAM user `iamuser_javed`.

## Solution
1. Log in to the AWS Management Console using the provided credentials.
2. Navigate to the **IAM** service.
3. In the left navigation pane, click on **IAM users**.
4. Click on the IAM user named `iamuser_javed`.
5. In the **Permissions** tab, click on **Add permissions**.
6. Choose **Attach policies directly**.
7. In the search bar, type `iampolicy_javed` to find the policy.
8. Select the checkbox next to `iampolicy_javed`.
9. Click on **Next: Review**.
10. Review the permissions and click on **Add permissions** to attach the policy to the user.

## Notes
- Individual IAM users can have specific permissions assigned to them through IAM policies, defining what actions the user can perform and on which AWS resources.
- https://docs.aws.amazon.com/IAM/latest/UserGuide/id_users_change-permissions.html
