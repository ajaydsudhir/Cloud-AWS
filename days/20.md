## Solution
1. Log in to the AWS Management Console using the provided credentials.
2. Navigate to the IAM service.
3. In the left navigation pane, click on "Roles" and then click on the "Create role" button.
4. Select "AWS service" as the trusted entity type and choose "EC2" as the use case. Click "Next: Permissions".
5. In the permissions policies search bar, search for the policy named `iampolicy_rose`. Select the policy and click "Next: Tags".
6. Enter `iamrole_rose` as the role name and click "Create role" to complete the process.
