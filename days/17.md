## Solution
1. Log in to the AWS Management Console using the provided credentials.
2. Navigate to the IAM service.
3. In the left navigation pane, click on "IAM User Groups" and then click the "Create Group" button.
4. Enter the group name `iamgroup_jim` and click "Next Step" to proceed to the permissions page.
5. On the permissions page, you can choose to attach existing policies to the group or skip this step for now. Click "Next Step" to continue.
6. Review the group details and click "Create Group" to finalize the creation of the IAM group.
