## Solution
1. Log in to the AWS Management Console using the provided credentials and navigate to the EC2 service in the `us-east-1` region.
2. In the left-hand menu, click on "Volumes" under the "Elastic Block Store" section.
3. Locate the volume named `datacenter-vol` in the list of volumes.
4. Select the volume and click on the "Actions" button, then choose "Create Snapshot" from the dropdown menu.
5. In the "Create Snapshot" dialog, enter `datacenter-vol-ss` as the name and `datacenter Snapshot` as the description.
6. Click "Create Snapshot" to initiate the snapshot creation process.
7. Monitor the status of the snapshot in the "Snapshots" section under "Elastic Block Store" until it shows as `completed`.
