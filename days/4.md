## Solution
1. Log in to the AWS Management Console using the provided credentials.
2. Navigate to the S3 service by searching for "S3" in the search bar and selecting it from the results.
3. In the S3 dashboard, locate the bucket named `nautilus-s3-13757` and click on its name to open the bucket details.
4. In the bucket details page, click on the "Properties" tab.
5. Scroll down to the "Bucket Versioning" section and click on the "Edit" button.
6. In the "Bucket Versioning" settings, select the option to "Enable" versioning.
7. Click the "Save changes" button to apply the versioning settings to the bucket.

## Notes
- `Versioning` is the process of keeping multiple versions of an object in the same bucket which allows you to preserve, retrieve, and restore every version of every object stored in your Amazon S3 bucket.
