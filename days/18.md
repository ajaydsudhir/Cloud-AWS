## Task
When establishing infrastructure on the AWS cloud, Identity and Access Management (IAM) is among the first and most critical services to configure. IAM facilitates the creation and management of user accounts, groups, roles, policies, and other access controls. ## Solution
1. Log in to the AWS Management Console using the provided credentials and navigate to the IAM service.
2. In the left navigation pane, click on "Policies" and then click the "Create policy" button.
3. Select the "JSON" tab and using the visual editor add all `Read` permissions for the EC2 service.
4. Click "Review policy", give the policy a name `iampolicy_rose`, and optionally provide a description. Then click "Create policy" to save the new policy. 

## Notes
- IAM policies are used to define permissions for users, groups, and roles in AWS. 
- A read-only policy for EC2 allows users to view resources without making any changes.
